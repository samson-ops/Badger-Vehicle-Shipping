FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Short answers below; the full FAQ covers everything else.

How much does it cost to ship a vehicle?

Price depends on distance, vehicle size, open or enclosed transport, and how soon you need pickup. The instant quote above is the all-in number, with no fees added later.

How long will my shipment take?

Transit time depends on the route. Most cross-country moves take 7 to 10 days; shorter lanes often deliver in 1 to 4. You get an estimated delivery window once a carrier is dispatched.

Is my vehicle insured during transport?

Yes. Every carrier in our network holds active cargo insurance, and we confirm it before dispatch. Coverage details are available before you book.

What is the difference between open and enclosed transport?

Open is the common, affordable option used for most vehicles. Enclosed adds a covered trailer that protects from weather and road debris, which is the usual choice for luxury, exotic and classic cars.

Do I have to pay a deposit upfront?

Getting a quote is free. Payment terms are confirmed at booking, before anything is charged.

Can you ship a vehicle that does not run?

Yes. Tell us at booking so we assign a carrier with a winch. Non-running vehicles carry a small adder that is included in your quote.
